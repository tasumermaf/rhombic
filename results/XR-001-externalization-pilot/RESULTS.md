## Verdict against the pre-registered predictions

**P1 (H-compaction) — CONFIRMED.** Structured typed blocks beat prose
summaries at matched token budgets, in every model, with the pre-registered
ordering R0 ≤ R2 < R1 intact everywhere:

| Model | R0 (no compaction) | R2 (structured) | R1 (prose) |
|---|---|---|---|
| gemma3:4b | 6.7% [3.4, 12.6] | 14.2% [9.0, 21.5] | 36.7% [28.6, 45.6] |
| qwen3:14b | 6.7% [3.4, 12.6] | 11.7% [7.1, 18.6] | 43.3% [34.8, 52.3] |
| qwen3-coder:30b | 0.0% [0.0, 3.1] | 2.5% [0.9, 7.1] | 29.2% [21.8, 37.8] |

(numeric-corruption rate, n = 120 probes per cell, 95% Wilson CI)

**Confirmatory test (pre-registered paired exact McNemar, R1 vs R2, pooled):
b = 11, c = 108, two-sided exact p = 3.524e-21, direction R2_better.**
Per-model: gemma3:4b p = 7.4e-06, qwen3-coder:30b p = 6.7e-08,
qwen3:14b p = 1.6e-09 — all R2_better. The falsifier (prose ≈ structured)
is decisively rejected.

**Matched-budget check — PASSED.** Mean realized compaction tokens:
R1 = 219.2, R2 = 221.4 (180 calls each; ~1% apart). The effect is format,
not budget.

**P2 (incident-signature union class, per amendment 1) — CONFIRMED.**
Signature errors (off_by_one ∪ conflation) pooled: R1 = 17 vs R2 = 4
(R0 = 4). Honest breakdown: the *bulk* of the prose deficit is not
near-miss drift but outright loss/mutation — R1's error mass is
other_wrong = 84 and omission = 30 against R2's 25 and 5. Prose compaction
mostly loses values; when it keeps them near-wrong, it conflates at ~4×
the structured rate (16 vs 3).

**P3 (exploratory, write-time decomposition) — SUPPORTED.** Final-state
retention across the 45 R1/R2 cells: R2 state blocks retained
898/900 entity values (99.8%); R1 prose retained 587/900 (65.2%). The
prose deficit enters predominantly at WRITE time. Caveats: prose scoring
is the pre-registered nearest-number heuristic (crude; likely understates
retained-value fidelity), and the R2 per-line counter shows a one-off
duplicate-line artifact (correct = 899 vs retained = 898) — exploratory
metric only, no confirmatory weight.

**Secondary (multi-hop completion, Q7–8):** R2 ≥ R1 in every model;
most dramatic on qwen3-coder:30b — R2 = 100% vs R1 = 46.7% (R0 = 100%).
gemma3:4b 50% vs 30%; qwen3:14b 53.3% vs 20%.

## Reading

At matched token budgets and temperature 0, replacing an agent's transcript
with a **prose summary** corrupts roughly **one numeric fact in three**
(pooled R1 = 131/360 = 36.4%), while a **typed state block** holds
corruption to **under one in ten** (pooled R2 = 34/360 = 9.4%; ceiling
R0 = 16/360 = 4.4%) — and the stronger the model, the closer structured
compaction gets to the no-compaction ceiling (30B: 2.5% vs 0%). This is
the agent-scaffolding analog of the published CoT-robustness finding:
externalized structure survives what lossy re-encoding destroys, and our
documented Feb 2026 summarization-corruption incident sits exactly in the
class this measures.

**This table is the baseline the Face-11 typed-adjacency compaction
prototype must beat** (plan item I5): its target is the R2 column, its
floor the R0 column, on harder-than-synthetic workloads.

## Scope caveats

One synthetic task family (numeric recall/combination under filler
pressure); three local models (4B–30B, one family duplicated); temperature
0 single-sample; compaction budget one point (~120 words / 256 tokens);
prose-stage scoring heuristic. None of these caveats touch the confirmatory
McNemar, which compares identical probes across regimes within model.
